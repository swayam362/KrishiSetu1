📐 How This Estimate Was Calculated: Expected Net Realization = Expected Revenue − Transport Cost − Other Selling Costs
Step 1: Expected Revenue
= Offered Price/kg × Quantity (e.g. ₹28 × 500 kg = ₹14,000)
Step 2: Transport Freight
= Distance (km) × Vehicle Rate + Loading/Unloading
Step 3: Other Selling Costs
= Packaging + APMC Mandi Cess + Perishability Spoilage Risk
Step 4: Net Realization
= Revenue − Transport − Other Costs (Actual money in your bank!)
