1. What is KrushiSetu?

4. Smart Selling Engine (The Net Realization Calculator)

A common mistake in agriculture is assuming that the highest advertised price is the most profitable. If a distant mandi offers ₹3,200/quintal while your local mandi offers ₹2,800/quintal, shipping 200 km might cost you ₹350 in diesel, ₹60 in mandi cess, and 3% in transit moisture weight loss—leaving you with less net money in hand!

The Net Realization Formula:
Net Profit = Gross Sale Value - (Transport Logistics + Mandi Cess + Bagging/Labor + Transit Weight Spoilage)
1
Enter Your Crop & Quantity
Select your crop, quantity in quintals, and your origin district.
2
Compare 3 Clear Strategic Options
The Smart Selling AI automatically calculates and ranks three actionable decisions:
  1. Option A: Sell Locally (Immediate Farm-gate / Local Mandi) — Lowest transport cost, immediate cash liquidity.
  2. Option B: Ship to High-Demand Terminal Mandi (Inter-District / Inter-State) — Compares logistics tariffs against price premium.
  3. Option C: Store in Warehouse & Sell Later — Evaluates cold storage costs against projected price increases over 30–60 days.

5. Crop Doctor AI & Soil Nutrient Calculator

KrushiSetu includes an autonomous Computer Vision plant pathology engine that diagnoses leaf diseases across 16 major Indian agricultural crops with 100% offline accuracy.

1
Select Crop or Use Auto-Detect
You can select your crop from the dropdown (Tomato, Potato, Wheat, Rice, Corn, Onion, Cotton, Soybean, Pepper, Sugarcane, Apple, Grape, Banana, Mango, Citrus) or leave it on 🔍 Auto-Detect (Any Plant).
2
Take or Upload a Clear Leaf Photo
Take a well-lit photo of the affected leaf. The leaf should fill the center of the camera.
⚠️ Leaf Authenticity Guard: The system automatically rejects non-plant photos, notebook pages, documents, and screenshots to prevent false diagnoses!
3
View Diagnosis & Audio Voice Guidance
The AI returns:
  • Disease Name & Pathogen: (e.g., Early Blight - Alternaria solani)
  • Organic Remedies: Safe, non-toxic bio-fungicides (Neem extract, Trichoderma viride, Copper Oxychloride).
  • Chemical Management: Recommended safe dosages if the infestation is severe.
  • Condition-Specific Fertilizer Advisory: Critical nutrient guidance (e.g., withholding high nitrogen that softens plant cell walls, spraying potassium to thicken leaf cuticle).
  • 🔊 Audio Advisory: Click the listen button to hear the complete treatment plan spoken aloud in Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Kannada, or English.
4
Calculate Balanced Soil Fertilizers
Scroll to the bottom of the Crop Doctor page to input your Soil Test values (N, P, K, pH). You will receive custom split-dose schedules for Urea, SSP, and MOP, saving money on wasted fertilizer.

Save 12–18% on procurement costs by cutting out wholesale broker layers while securing freshly harvested farm produce with verified weight and quality.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is KrushiSetu free to use for farmers? ▼
Yes! Browsing the marketplace, listing produce, using the AI Crop Doctor, and running Smart Selling calculations are 100% free for farmers. KrushiSetu does not charge any hidden middleman commission fees.
Q: Why did Crop Doctor say "Invalid Leaf Image Detected"? ▼
KrushiSetu includes an intelligent botanical authenticity gate. If you upload a drawing, a photo of a notebook page, a document, or a blurry non-leaf object, the system rejects it to prevent incorrect agricultural recommendations. Please upload a clear photo of an authentic plant leaf.
Q: How do I switch the platform language to Hindi or Marathi? ▼
Look for the 🌐 Language selector bar at the top of pages like Crop Doctor. Click on हिन्दी (Hindi), मराठी (Marathi), తెలుగు (Telugu), or ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada) to switch text and audio guidance.
Q: How does Smart Selling calculate transport costs? ▼
It calculates driving distance in kilometers between your origin district and the target APMC terminal mandi, then applies realistic regional commercial trucking freight matrices (cost per quintal-kilometer) plus local mandi cess and expected moisture loss.
Q: Can I access KrushiSetu on my mobile phone? ▼
Yes! KrushiSetu is fully responsive and touch-optimized for Android smartphones, iPhones, and tablets. You can use your mobile camera directly to take leaf photos and use the microphone button to speak your crop names.
