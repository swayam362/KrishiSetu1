Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

Confidence: 94% Match
Moderate Severity

Detected Symptoms:

  • Concentric dark brown rings on lower leaves
  • Yellow halo surrounding necrotic leaf spots
  • Early leaf drop and foliar drying

🌿 Recommended Organic Treatment:

Spray Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5g/L water. Prune infected bottom foliage and destroy them away from field. Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation.

🧪 Chemical Treatment (If severe):

Apply Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1ml/L at 10-day intervals.
